# Official Language and Professional Writing

## Why this matters
Government and consulting work is judged not only by technical quality but also by the quality of communication.

## Core rules
- begin with purpose
- write for the audience
- separate facts from recommendations
- keep tone formal, respectful, and neutral
- be clear, concise, and action-oriented

## Examples
### Poor
We need this to happen quickly and we are hoping it will be approved.

### Professional
This proposal is submitted for consideration and approval of the departmental digital enhancement initiative to improve citizen access and streamline service delivery.

## Exercise
Rewrite a short informal note into formal government language.

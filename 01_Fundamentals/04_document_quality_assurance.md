# Document Quality Assurance Checklist

## Before sending any document, check:
- spelling and grammar
- consistency of numbering and formatting
- logic and flow of argument
- evidence and financial accuracy
- clarity of action requested
- final sign-off and version history

## QA template
| Section | Check |
|---|---|
| Purpose | Does the objective read clearly? |
| Tone | Is the language formal and neutral? |
| Facts | Are claims supported? |
| Structure | Is the document logically ordered? |
| Action | Is the decision or follow-up clear? |

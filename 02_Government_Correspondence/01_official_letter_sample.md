# Official Letter Sample

## Purpose
Used for formal communication between government offices, departments, and agencies.

## Structure
- Header
- Subject
- Date and reference
- Body
- Action requested
- Closing

## Sample
**To**
The Principal Secretary
Department of Electronics & IT
Government of Odisha

**Subject:** Request for approval of digital service delivery project

Sir,

This is to respectfully submit the proposal for approval of the Integrated Citizen Service Delivery Platform for the Department of Revenue and Disaster Management. The project intends to streamline public service access, improve service turnaround time, and enhance digital monitoring.

The preliminary review indicates that the conceptualisation and planning phase can be completed within 45 days and implementation can proceed in a phased manner.

Kindly accord approval for the preliminary planning and budgetary sanction as per departmental procedure.

Thanking you,

[Name]
[Designation]

# D.O. Letter Sample

## Purpose
Used for personal but official communication between officers.

## Structure
- Salutation
- Purpose statement
- Context
- Request or action
- Courtesy closing

## Sample
**D.O. No.:** 17/2026

Dear Shri Sahu,

I am writing to request your kind review of the draft concept note on the departmental citizen service delivery initiative. We are in the final stage of internal vetting and would value your guidance on the scope and implementation sequencing.

The proposal is structured around service mapping, workflow automation, and governance oversight. A detailed discussion may be useful before the next review meeting.

I would be grateful for your suggestions and directions.

With regards,
[Name]

# Office Memorandum Sample

## Purpose
To communicate routine decisions, instructions, or clarifications.

## Sample
To: Head, IT and Digital Services Cell
From: Project Management Unit
Date: 12 November 2026
Subject: Submission of project status and risk update

This memorandum is issued to inform the office that the project status review for the Integrated Citizen Service Delivery Platform is scheduled for 18 November 2026. The review will focus on implementation progress, outstanding risks, and required decisions on administrative approvals.

The project team is requested to submit the updated risk register, milestone status, and pending approval items in advance for discussion.

This is issued for necessary action and record.

# Office Order Sample

## Purpose
To formally declare an assignment or administrative directive.

## Sample
Office Order No. 21/2026
Date: 15 November 2026

In view of the requirement for monitoring and coordination of the Digital Service Delivery Project, the following officers are assigned responsibilities:

1. Project Lead: Mr. A. Das
2. Technical Lead: Mr. B. Mohanty
3. MIS and Reporting Officer: Ms. C. Patnaik

The officers shall coordinate with the department, vendor team, and PMU for implementation monitoring and issue resolution.

This order is effective immediately.

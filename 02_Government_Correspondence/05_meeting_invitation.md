# Meeting Invitation Sample

## Purpose
To invite officers or stakeholders to a formal review meeting.

## Sample
Subject: Invitation to Project Review Meeting on Citizen Service Delivery Platform

Dear Sir / Madam,

This is to invite you to a review meeting on the Integrated Citizen Service Delivery Platform to be held on 20 November 2026 at 3:00 PM in the Conference Room, Secretariat Building.

The agenda will cover implementation status, key issues, resource requirements, and decision points.

Your participation is requested for effective project coordination and governance.

Kindly confirm your availability.

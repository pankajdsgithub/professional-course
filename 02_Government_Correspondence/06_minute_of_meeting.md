# Minutes of Meeting Sample

## Purpose
To record decisions, issues, and action items.

## Example areas
- meeting title and date
- participants list
- agenda points
- decisions taken
- action responsibilities
- due dates

## Sample excerpt
Meeting: Project Review Meeting
Date: 20 November 2026

Decision 1: The project team shall submit a revised implementation schedule within three days.
Decision 2: Data migration checklist shall be approved before UAT commencement.
Action Owner: Project Manager
Due Date: 23 November 2026

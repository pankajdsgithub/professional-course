# Procurement Lifecycle in Government Projects

## Lifecycle stages
1. need assessment and project approval
2. budget and planning
3. pre-procurement preparation
4. tender drafting and publication
5. pre-bid clarification
6. bid submission and opening
7. technical evaluation
8. financial evaluation
9. negotiation and award
10. contract execution and monitoring
11. implementation review and closure

## Considerations
- GFR and financial rules
- CVC principles
- GeM requirements where applicable
- department-specific administrative norms
- transparency, fairness, and record maintenance

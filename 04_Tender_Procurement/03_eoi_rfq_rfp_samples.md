# EOI, RFQ, and RFP Distinction

## Expression of Interest (EOI)
Used to assess market interest and vendor capability before formal procurement.

## Request for Quotation (RFQ)
Used for straightforward procurement where price and service quality are primary factors.

## Request for Proposal (RFP)
Used for complex technology projects requiring technical and commercial assessment.

## Example use
- EOI: vendor pre-qualification
- RFQ: hardware or software purchases
- RFP: system implementation and support engagement

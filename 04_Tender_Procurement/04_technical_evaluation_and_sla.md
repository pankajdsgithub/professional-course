# Technical Evaluation and SLA

## Technical evaluation report
This report summarises whether a bidder meets the technical requirements and implementation capability expected for the project.

## Evaluation focus
- compliance to scope
- technical approach
- implementation plan
- architecture fit
- team capability
- prior experience

## Service Level Agreement (SLA)
SLA defines uptime, support response, resolution time, and reporting obligations.

## Example requirement
The vendor shall respond to critical incidents within 2 hours and restore service within 8 hours for critical production issues.

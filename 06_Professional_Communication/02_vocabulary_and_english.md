# Executive Vocabulary and Professional English

## Useful phrases
- For kind consideration and approval
- This matter is submitted for review and guidance
- The project is progressing as per plan subject to timely clearance
- The key issues requiring intervention are as follows
- We would appreciate your direction on the next steps

## Communication style
- respectful
- concise
- specific
- disciplined
- evidence-based

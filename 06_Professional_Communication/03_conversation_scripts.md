# Conversation Scripts and Role Play

## Scenario: giving a project update
Sir, the project is progressing in accordance with the approved milestones. Two critical items remain: data migration validation and vendor onboarding approval. We have submitted the revised timeline and are working on the mitigation plan. The recommended action is to grant the UAT extension and continue vendor coordination on the remaining dependencies.

## Scenario: handling delay
We understand the concern regarding the current timeline. To ensure successful delivery, we recommend the following sequence of actions: approve the revised schedule, complete validation on the remaining data points, and proceed with the remaining configuration under the approved governance structure.

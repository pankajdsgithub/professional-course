# Meeting Scenarios for Government Work

## Scenario 1: monthly project review
Objective: review schedule, cost, risk, and outstanding actions.

## Scenario 2: departmental IT assessment meeting
Objective: identify current gaps and future technology priorities.

## Scenario 3: delay escalation meeting
Objective: address slippage and obtain administrative direction.

## Scenario 4: vendor performance review
Objective: assess service quality and SLA compliance.

## Scenario 5: cybersecurity review
Objective: check access, infrastructure, and remediation priorities.

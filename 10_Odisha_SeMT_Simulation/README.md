# Module 10: Odisha SeMT Practical Simulation

## Objective
This module is the capstone-style simulation of a realistic state government digital transformation project. It ties together all prior learning into one end-to-end consulting workflow for a fictional Odisha department.

## Case study: Integrated Citizen Service Delivery Platform
A fictional Odisha government department is facing delays and inconsistencies in citizen service delivery due to fragmented manual workflows, disconnected departmental systems, and inconsistent service tracking. The department plans to implement a unified digital platform that will enable citizens to submit service requests, track status, and access relevant service updates through a standard process.

## Project purpose
- improve citizen experience and access
- reduce manual processing delays
- create digital service workflow transparency
- improve departmental monitoring and reporting
- standardise service delivery procedures

## Stakeholders
- department secretary or principal secretary
- nodal officer / project owner
- PMU and project management lead
- IT and digital services team
- district functionaries and service officers
- technical vendor
- cybersecurity and monitoring teams
- SeMT review team or equivalent state-level digital governance team

## Project scope
The platform will include:
- citizen request submission and tracking
- workflow automation for service requests
- document and record management
- dashboard and MIS reporting
- role-based access for department users
- integration with relevant external systems where required
- escalation and service response monitoring

## Lifecycle stages
1. Initial departmental meeting and project framing
2. requirement gathering and current-state analysis
3. preparation of concept note and problem statement
4. preparation of DPR, financial estimate, and implementation plan
5. RFP drafting and procurement planning
6. technical and financial evaluation of bids
7. vendor selection and contract finalisation
8. implementation governance and project monitoring
9. UAT, issue resolution, and operational readiness
10. go-live, closure, and performance assessment

## Practical outputs to generate
- problem statement note
- concept note
- project charter
- DPR summary
- financial estimate note
- bid evaluation report
- vendor scorecard
- project status report
- UAT report and closure note
- final executive review deck

## Why this is important
This case is designed to simulate real government consulting work. It helps the learner connect writing, documentation, evaluation, reporting, meeting behaviour, and stakeholder communication into one coherent project lifecycle.

## Best practice reminder
The learner should maintain continuity across all documents, keep assumptions explicit, and ensure that the tone and structure are suitable for governance-level review.

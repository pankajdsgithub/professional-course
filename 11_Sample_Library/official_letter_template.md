# Official Letter Template

## Purpose
Used for formal communication with departments and agencies.

## When used
For approval requests, sharing decisions, or requesting information.

## Structure
1. Header
2. Subject
3. Date
4. Reference
5. Salutation
6. Purpose
7. Background
8. Action requested
9. Closing

## Sample
To
The Principal Secretary
Department of Electronics & IT
Government of Odisha

Subject: Request for approval of project initiation for e-Services portal enhancement

Sir,

This is to respectfully submit the proposal for approval of the project initiation phase for the e-Services portal enhancement initiative. The initiative is intended to improve citizen access to departmental services and ensure reduced turnaround time.

The initial review indicates that the project can be implemented in phases, beginning with requirement analysis, technology architecture, and service mapping.

Kindly approve the initiation of the project and the associated preliminary cost estimate for the planning stage.

Thanking you,

[Name]
[Designation]

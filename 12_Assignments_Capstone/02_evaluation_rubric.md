# Capstone Evaluation Rubric

| Criterion | Weight |
|---|---|
| professionalism and tone | 20% |
| structure and completeness | 20% |
| evidence and logic | 20% |
| decision orientation | 15% |
| presentation quality | 15% |
| governance fit | 10% |

A learner passes when the final package is clear, evidence-based, professional, and ready for presentation to a senior government audience.

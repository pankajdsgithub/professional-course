# 12-Week Learning Roadmap

This roadmap is designed as a structured professional training cycle. It progresses from foundational documentation skills to executive-level communication, procurement literacy, and complete government project delivery capability.

## Weekly study structure
Each week follows the same learning rhythm:
- 30-minute daily practice
- 1 core concept lesson
- 1 realistic document or slide exercise
- 1 quality improvement drill
- 1 assignment or role-play session

## Week-by-week plan

### Week 1: Professional Documentation Fundamentals
Focus:
- writing for government and consulting contexts
- structure, clarity, tone, and formatting
- MS Word essentials and document quality review

Deliverables:
- one office note
- one cover note
- one refined memo
- document QA checklist

### Week 2: Government & Official Correspondence
Focus:
- letters, memos, office orders, reminders, and notices
- formal email and escalation communication
- minutes, ATR, and decision tracking

Deliverables:
- 3 letters
- 2 office order samples
- 1 MoM and 1 ATR

### Week 3: Project Management Documentation I
Focus:
- concept note, project charter, project proposal
- executive and project-level problem framing

Deliverables:
- concept note
- project charter
- project proposal summary

### Week 4: Project Management Documentation II
Focus:
- DPR, BRD, SRS, FRD, and status reporting
- solution design and governance documentation

Deliverables:
- DPR outline
- BRD
- SRS
- project status report

### Week 5: Tender & Procurement Documentation I
Focus:
- EOI, RFQ, RFP, and bid preparation flow
- SoW and ToR drafting

Deliverables:
- EOI draft
- RFQ sample
- RFP skeleton
- SoW and ToR

### Week 6: Tender & Procurement Documentation II
Focus:
- bid evaluation, vendor assessment, SLA, and MoU
- procurement compliance awareness

Deliverables:
- technical evaluation matrix
- financial evaluation note
- vendor report
- SLA and MoU draft

### Week 7: Executive Presentation Mastery
Focus:
- slide design, flow, and storytelling
- dashboards, charts, infographics, and strategic presentation structure

Deliverables:
- 3 executive decks
- 1 dashboard presentation
- one strategic roadmap deck

### Week 8: Professional Communication & English
Focus:
- speaking with confidence and brevity
- formal email etiquette and executive summaries
- difficult stakeholder communication

Deliverables:
- 5 professional emails
- 3 spoken briefing scripts
- 3 role-play exercises

### Week 9: Meeting & Stakeholder Management
Focus:
- creating agendas, recording decisions, and writing minutes
- follow-up tracking and escalation communication

Deliverables:
- 3 agenda drafts
- 3 MoMs
- 1 issue escalation note
- 1 action tracker

### Week 10: Data Analysis & Reporting
Focus:
- Excel reporting, KPI logic, and management dashboards
- project performance tracking and monitoring

Deliverables:
- project schedule tracker
- budget dashboard
- KPI status board
- risk dashboard

### Week 11: Advanced Digital Tools and AI-based Support
Focus:
- using MS Office, Google Workspace, Teams, and AI tools responsibly
- generating, checking, and refining draft work with digital assistance

Deliverables:
- AI prompt bank
- draft rewrite workflow
- executive-summary generator process

### Week 12: Final Capstone and Professional Certification Project
Focus:
- integrate all skills into one realistic project submission
- final review, polishing, and decision-oriented communication

Deliverables:
- concept note
- DPR
- RFP
- project plan
- risk register
- 3 MoMs
- progress report
- final executive presentation
- 5 emails
- final review deck

## Daily practice model
- 10 minutes: professional English and vocabulary
- 10 minutes: documentation writing
- 5 minutes: presentation rehearsal
- 5 minutes: stakeholder communication drill

## Quality checkpoint at end of every week
Each learner should review:
- clarity of writing
- professionalism of tone
- completeness of structure
- adequacy of follow-up actions
- relevance to the government consulting context

## Expected final outcome
A learner should move from basic drafting to senior-consultant-level performance in official communication, reporting, presentation, governance, and project coordination.

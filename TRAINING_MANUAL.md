# Training Manual for Professional Government Consulting Course

## 1. Purpose of the programme
This training manual is designed to help learners develop the professional communication, documentation, presentation, and reporting skills expected of a senior consultant in public-sector and e-Governance assignments.

## 2. Target audience
- project analysts and consultants
- government officers working in digital transformation
- PMU teams and implementation support personnel
- professionals handling e-Governance and state digital programmes
- people supporting department-level project coordination

## 3. Training outcome
At the end of this programme, the learner should be able to:
- write and review official documents with confidence
- prepare project documentation for internal and external review
- produce executive presentations and project dashboards
- manage stakeholder communication and senior-level meetings
- prepare procurement and contract-related materials
- handle risk, delay, and escalation communication professionally

## 4. Teaching method
The course uses a practical learning method:
- start with fundamentals
- explain in simple Hindi/Hinglish and formal English
- show poor, good, and excellent examples
- use real-world government situations and realistic examples
- give templates that can be reused in actual work
- assign follow-up tasks and review quality after each module

## 5. Learning standards
Every document should follow these standards:
- clear purpose
- structured format
- professional tone
- factual and evidence-based content
- actionable decision points
- appropriate audience alignment
- clean versioning and review trail

## 6. Daily discipline for improvement
A learner should practice the following each day:
- write one formal note or email
- review one sample document
- improve one weak paragraph into a polished version
- build one slide or one dashboard element
- role-play one stakeholder communication scenario

## 7. Assessment framework
A learner is considered ready when the work demonstrates:
- strong communication clarity
- confidence in formal tone and structure
- proper use of templates and formats
- evidence-based reasoning and recommendations
- professional handling of meetings and follow-up
- suitable communication for senior decision-makers

## 8. Standard checklist for every document
Before finalising a document, review:
- purpose and objective
- audience and tone
- format and numbering
- grammar and spelling
- facts and financial numbers
- action requested or decision required
- annexures and references
- final approval/status notes

## 9. Professional writing principles
- begin with objective and context
- avoid informal or emotional language
- do not mix facts, opinion, and recommendation in one paragraph
- keep recommendations direct and actionable
- maintain clarity, moderation, and professionalism in every line

## 10. Sample government consulting scenario
A department is implementing a digital citizen service platform. The consultant must:
- prepare a note for internal review
- prepare a concept note and DPR
- create procurement documents
- prepare presentation decks for senior officers
- prepare minutes and follow-up actions
- monitor milestone and risk status
- communicate changes and escalations professionally

## 11. Professional success behaviours
- be prepared before every meeting
- know the decision points in advance
- keep a clean action tracker
- do not send documents without checking facts
- ensure the final message is simple, precise, and senior-friendly

## 12. Final training goal
The learner should be able to step into a senior consulting role and contribute meaningfully to documentation, meeting management, reporting, presentations, procurement support, stakeholder communication, and project governance in government technology assignments.

# Week 1 Assignments and Evaluation Framework

## Assignment 1: Note drafting
Write a one-page note or office memorandum on a digital service enhancement initiative.

## Assignment 2: Rewriting exercise
Convert poor-quality language into a professional government note.

## Assignment 3: Document QA exercise
Check a draft for spelling, formatting, numbering, consistency, and clarity.

## Evaluation scorecard
| Criterion | Weight |
|---|---|
| clarity | 20% |
| tone and professionalism | 20% |
| structure | 20% |
| grammar and formatting | 20% |
| actionability | 20% |
